# PharmIQ Engineering & Pedagogical Roadmap

## Milestone 1: Pilot Validation & Pharmacokinetics Wedge

### Phase 1: Phase A — PK & Calculation Engine Proof (Weeks 1–3) [ACTIVE]
- **Goal:** Deliver deterministic Cockcroft-Gault DAG calculation engine, Socratic diagnostic dialogue FSM, Turkish PK seed items, Supabase schema with RLS, and mobile-first Ant Design drill workspace for Biruni University 3rd-year cohort.
- **Decision Gate A:**
  - $\ge 60\%$ pilot completion across daily sessions.
  - $\ge 85\%$ human pharmacist alignment on classified error nodes.
  - $0\%$ arithmetic/calculation errors produced by deterministic engine.

### Phase 2: Phase B — Course Companion & Syllabus Ingestion (Weeks 4–7)
- **Goal:** Automated slide/PDF ingestion, "Professor Pearl" extraction, expanded 1-compartment PK ($k_e, t_{1/2}, V_d, CL, AUC, C_{\text{ss}}, R$), and exam-sprint timed mocks.
- **Decision Gate B:**
  - $\ge 40\%$ 4-week active retention.
  - $< 2\%$ flagged question rate from QA pipeline.

### Phase 3: Phase C — Multi-Course Rollout & Licensure Prep (Post-Gate B)
- **Goal:** Multi-compartment ODE solvers, full patient case simulator, multi-university scaling, and commercial payment gateways (iyzico/Paddle).
