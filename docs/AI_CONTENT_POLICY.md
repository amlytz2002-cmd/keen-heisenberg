# CENNA.live AI Content, Safety & Grounding Policy

**Version:** 1.0 (Pilot Implementation)  
**Standard:** Evidence-Based Pedagogical AI  
**Scope:** LLM Extraction, Concept Structuring, Question Generation, and Socratic Scaffolding  

---

## 1. Foundational Philosophy

In CENNA.live, Artificial Intelligence serves as a precision instructional drafting assistant for educators—never an autonomous, unsupervised authority.

Educational materials generated by AI are treated as **untrusted drafts** until an authorized human instructor inspects, verifies, and approves them.

---

## 2. Source Grounding & Paragraph Provenance

Every concept card, question stem, distractor option, and explanation produced by the extraction pipeline must adhere to the following strict provenance rules:
1. **Source Attribution:** Must reference a registered document in the course repository.
2. **Coordinate Granularity:** Must record the specific `page_number` and `paragraph_index` of the source text chunk.
3. **Chunk Verification:** When viewed in the **Content Review Workspace**, the exact excerpt from the source chunk is rendered side-by-side with the drafted question for immediate factual audit.
4. **Zero Out-of-Corpus Facts:** Models are explicitly instructed never to fabricate facts, clinical dosing guidelines, or formulas outside the provided chunk context.

---

## 3. Mandatory Publication Gate & Status Lifecycle

AI-generated items follow a non-bypassable four-state lifecycle:

```
  [Extraction Pipeline]
           │
           ▼
    [needs_review]  <-- Strictly invisible to students in DB & API
           │
     ┌─────┴──────────────────────┐
     ▼                            ▼
[approved] (Optional)          [rejected] (Archived with Reason)
     │
     ▼
[published]         <-- Visible to students in active study sessions
```

- **Rule 1:** Ordinary students cannot view or query any concept or question where `status != 'published'`.
- **Rule 2:** The system will never automatically promote a `needs_review` item to `published` without explicit human instructor action.
- **Rule 3:** If an instructor edits a question during review (`edit_and_approve`), the updated stem, options, and explanation overwrite the AI draft and the modification is logged.

---

## 4. Inference Parameters & Anti-Hallucination Controls

To minimize variance and hallucination risk:
- **Temperature:** Clamped to `0.1`–`0.2` for concept extraction and question authoring.
- **Output Schema:** Enforced via strict Zod schemas defining expected JSON structure.
- **Prompt Sanitization:** Document chunks are scrubbed of markdown injection vectors and system command patterns before entering the prompt context.
- **Cascade Fallback:** If upstream models fail or return schema-invalid responses (such as premature answer disclosure in hint ladders), execution falls back to deterministic rule preceptors.

---

## 5. Medical & Clinical Disclaimer

1. **Academic Use Only:** CENNA.live materials are engineered solely for academic study, retrieval practice, and exam preparation in accredited educational institutions.
2. **No Clinical Reliance:** Questions, explanations, and hints must not be used for direct clinical patient care, diagnostic decision-making, or prescription verification.
