# CENNA.live — Campus Pilot Feedback Survey & Evaluation Framework

**Purpose:** Evaluate learning efficacy, source citation trust, and platform usability among participating pharmacy students and instructors.  
**Privacy Notice:** This survey is strictly academic, voluntary, and anonymized. No clinical records or sensitive identifiers are collected.
